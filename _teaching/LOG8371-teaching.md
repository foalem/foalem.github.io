Instructor for the **LOG8371 Software Quality Engineering** course.
Topics covered include: Basic concepts and relevant definitions related to software quality, quality assurance, testing, quality engineering, and quality planning. Defect prevention and classification. Fault tolerance. Software reliability engineering. Quality models. Comparison of different quality assurance techniques. Software development process improvement. Software and process measurement. Risk identification for measurable quality improvement.
